Atomic Habits

Book Name: Atomic Habits

Author Name: James Clear

Published Year: 2018

Genre: Fiction / Adventure

About the Book

Atomic Habits is a self-help book written by James Clear and published in 2018. The book explains how small changes in our daily habits can lead to big improvements in our lives over time. It teaches readers how to build good habits, break bad habits, stay consistent, and become more productive. The main message of the book is that small improvements repeated every day can create great results in the long term.

Why I Like This Book

I like Atomic Habits because it teaches simple and practical ways to improve our daily life. The book helps me understand the importance of discipline, consistency, and small positive changes. I also like it because its lessons can be applied to studies, work, and personal goals.

“You do not rise to the level of your goals. You fall to the level of your systems.”
— James Clear, Atomic Habits
